# Instructions for AI assistants

If the person wants to install, update, customize, troubleshoot or uninstall Camybrith Cooking,
or root their Monsieur Cuisine Smart, follow `assistant/camybrith-installer/SKILL.md`: it guides
them step by step, with the safety rules and checkpoints. The procedure itself is
`docs/GUIDE.md` (French) and `docs/GUIDE.en.md` (English).

If the person wants to work on the code, see `README.en.md` (build, layout) and `docs/SMART.md`
(protocol notes). Never commit `outils/robot.conf`, `modules/adb-wifi/adb_keys`, APKs, personal
sounds or recipes.
